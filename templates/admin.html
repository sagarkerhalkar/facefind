Step 1 - Add an album

Guests who use the general link choose their dates; this album is searched when its date is inside them.

Option A: paste a Google Drive folder link in the box below (one link per line). The photos are scanned on Drive itself - nothing is copied to this computer.
Option B: press Choose folder and pick a folder on this computer or on any connected drive. You can mix both.
For photos that are on Google Drive, use the link (Option A), not the G: drive letter: through G: Windows downloads every photo first, which is slow.

    Photo types read: JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, TIFF, HEIC (iPhone) and camera RAW files (ARW, CR2, CR3, NEF, DNG, RAF, RW2, ORF and more).
