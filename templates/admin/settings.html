Looking before signing in

With this on, anyone who has the link can see previews of a person by uploading that person's picture. Turn it off for private events.

Match strictness

Higher = fewer photos of other people, but some of the guest's own photos may be missed. To choose: use "Test with my own selfie" on an album; each photo shows its score. If another person's photo shows 0.52, set this to 0.55. No new scan is needed.
